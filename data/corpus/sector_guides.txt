Sector guide: Cyber and digital (Cheltenham).
Gloucestershire is a UK cyber hub anchored by GCHQ and a dense SME network (including CyNam). School leavers often enter via Level 3/4 cyber or digital apprenticeships. University leavers target software, SOC analyst, threat intelligence, and secure engineering roles. Useful preparation: networking basics, Python, TryHackMe labs, GitHub portfolio, and attending local cyber meetups. Emphasise ethics, curiosity, and continuous learning.

Sector guide: Aerospace and advanced manufacturing.
The Staverton / Tewkesbury / Gloucester corridor hosts GE Aerospace, Moog, Dowty, Safran and precision firms like Renishaw and Spirax. Common entry routes are engineering and manufacturing apprenticeships plus STEM graduate schemes. Useful preparation: Maths/Physics/Engineering Level 3, CAD, workshop safety, and a practical project (sensor build, CAD assembly, quality case study).

Sector guide: Agri-tech and food.
Cirencester and Hartpury ecosystems (RAU, Farm491, Hartpury, Campden BRI) connect farming, food science and technology. Entry routes include land-based apprenticeships, food lab technician roles, and graduate agribusiness/tech posts. Useful preparation: outdoor practical experience, biology/chemistry, sustainability projects, and interest in real farm problems.

Sector guide: Health and public service.
NHS Gloucestershire and Gloucestershire County Council are large local employers with apprenticeships, support-worker routes, digital, admin and graduate schemes. Values-based recruitment matters. Useful preparation: volunteering, care experience, first aid, reflective examples of teamwork and compassion, plus Level 2/3 English and Maths.
